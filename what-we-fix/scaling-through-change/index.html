What We Fix

Scaling Through Change

An acquisition, a lost supplier, or a growth plan with seven priorities. The business still has to run.

How we approach it

Keep the company running while the plan changes underneath it.

  1. 1

    Separate the symptom from the cause

    Gap analysis before blame: find what is actually breaking, not who.

  2. 2

    Design the target, then work back

    Plan for the futures you might face and turn each gap into something to build.

  3. 3

    Run the fix and the business together

    Triage by hand while the real fix lands, then set a cadence the team keeps after we leave.
