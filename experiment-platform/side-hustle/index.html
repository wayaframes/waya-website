Frequently asked

Before you apply

My side hustle barely makes money. Is it too early?

No. The program is built for the stage before product-market fit. You do not need revenue or a finished product. You need something you are serious about and the willingness to talk to customers every week.

I was just laid off. Should I do this now?

If you are weighing a business against another job, the 12 weeks give you evidence for that decision. Many people run the program while they interview, because the answer can be go, pivot or stop, and all three are useful.

What if the answer is no?

Then you found out in 12 weeks and at a fixed cost, instead of after burning your savings. Every experiment has a pass line and a drop-dead date, so a no is a clear result, not a vague disappointment.

How much time does it take each week?

Two touchpoints a week: one group session and one private hour with a founder coach. Between sessions you run experiments and talk to customers, so expect real work outside the calls.

What does it cost, and when does it start?

$3,000 for the full 12-week program. The first cohort starts in mid-November 2026. Seats are limited and filled by a short application and a conversation.
