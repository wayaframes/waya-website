Frequently asked

Before you apply

How much time does it take each week?

Two touchpoints a week: one group session and one private hour with a founder coach. Between sessions you run experiments and talk to customers, so expect real work outside the calls.

Can this work around a family schedule?

The program is two touchpoints a week plus experiments and customer conversations in between. You decide how to fit the in-between work into your week. Ask about session times in your conversation after you apply.

Do I have to leave my job or stop what I am doing now?

No. The program asks for one group session and one private hour a week, plus experiments and customer conversations in between. The 12 weeks give you evidence to decide how and when to make a bigger move.

I have no revenue and no finished product. Is it too early?

No. The program is built for before product-market fit. You do not need revenue or a finished product. You need an idea you are serious about and the willingness to talk to customers every week.

What if it turns out not to be a fit?

Then you found out in 12 weeks and at a fixed cost, before you built anything. Every experiment has a pass line and a drop-dead date, so a no is a clear result, and your savings stay available for the idea that works.

What does it cost, and when does it start?

$3,000 for the full 12-week program. The first cohort starts in mid-November 2026. Seats are limited and filled by a short application and a conversation.
