How Waya Handles Your Data

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This page describes what Waya stores from your sessions, where it is stored, who can access it, how it is protected, and how to get a copy or have it deleted. The Privacy Policy is the governing document, and this page is consistent with it.

What we store

CategoryWhat it is
AccountYour email address and an identifier from our sign-in provider.
Session contentThe notes you provide at each methodology step, stored verbatim: from business methodologies (problem statements, root causes, gap lists, business cases) and from personal methodologies like Ikigai (your reflections about yourself). Also the confirmations you approve at checkpoints and the close-out record of each session.
Operational dataTimestamps, which step was called, response times, which AI app you connected with and its declared capabilities, session tokens, and security flags from abuse detection.
Request logsStandard server logs at our hosting providers (IP address, request path, status).

We store session content verbatim so you can resume a session and carry prior work into later ones.

What we don't do

  • We never train AI models on your content. The AI assistant you use is your own client, under your agreement with its provider; we don't operate it.
  • We don't sell your data, share it for advertising, or show ads.
  • We don't move your content anywhere except the systems listed below.

Where it is stored and who can access it

Everything above is stored in two production PostgreSQL databases, one for Waya OS and one for Waya Pre-PMF, hosted by Supabase in the United States. Access is limited:

  • There is no public access path. Only the server, and the Waya team through the database console, can reach the databases. Row-level security is enabled, and the anonymous and authenticated database roles hold no grants, so no public API key or client role can read your content.
  • Application hosting is Vercel and sign-in is WorkOS. When our team reviews session records, it sometimes uses AI tools, only under business terms that bar the provider from training on our data. The website runs separately. The Privacy Policy lists the providers we use to run the services and the website.

People on the Waya team can read session records, including your notes, when they review how a session went, sometimes with the help of AI tools, to improve the methodology. This review is internal and limited to our team, and the Privacy Policy describes it.

How it is protected

  • Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest (database-level encryption).
  • Separate environments: staging and production are distinct deployments with distinct databases, and we do not test against production data.
  • Change management: every change ships through a reviewed pull request, and the full history is version-controlled.
  • Audit trail: every methodology call is logged with its timestamp and outcome.
  • Abuse detection: automated flagging of scraping patterns.

We model these controls on SOC 2. We do not hold a SOC 2 certification or any other third-party attestation; if that changes, we will update this page.

Getting a copy of your data, or deleting it

Email info@wayaframes.com and, within 30 days of verifying the request, we will:

  • Export: send you a machine-readable copy of everything keyed to your account, including your account record, sessions, notes, and close-out records.
  • Delete: erase your account and everything keyed to it, including your identity at our sign-in provider. Residual copies in encrypted backups expire on a rolling schedule within a further 30 days.

We keep your content for the life of your account and delete it when you ask. Apart from those backups, we keep no other copies.
