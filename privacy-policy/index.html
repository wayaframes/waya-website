Waya Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Waya is operated by Waterbear LLC ("Waya," "we," "us"). We provide facilitated business methodologies through our services — Waya OS and Waya Pre-PMF — and we run the website at wayaframes.com.

This policy explains what we collect, why, and what your rights are. The services and the website collect different things, so they get separate sections. Questions or requests: info@wayaframes.com.

The services

You use Waya through an AI assistant (such as Claude) that connects to our servers. Three kinds of information reach us:

Your account. When you sign in, our identity provider (WorkOS) gives us your email address and an account identifier. That is the only account information we hold about you.

Your session content. Waya works by walking you through a methodology step by step. The notes you provide at each step are stored verbatim. They come from business methodologies (problem statements, root causes, gap lists, business cases) and from personal methodologies like Ikigai (your reflections about yourself). We also store the confirmations you approve at checkpoints and the close-out record of each session. This is your working material, and we treat it that way — see How Waya Handles Your Data for exactly where it lives and who can reach it.

Operational data. Timestamps, which methodology step was called, response times, which AI app you connected with and its declared capabilities, session tokens, security flags from our abuse detection, and standard server request logs.

Where the AI fits

The AI assistant you talk to is your client, operating under your agreement with its provider — we don't run it. It sends your notes to our servers on your behalf, and our servers send methodology back. We never use your content to train AI models.

If you work with us as a consulting client

We ask consulting clients to connect Waya under their own account, so everything in this policy applies to them directly. When that isn't possible, our team runs the sessions under a Waya account on your behalf. The same commitments apply to that content: it is stored and protected as described here, only our team reviews it, and we delete it when you ask.

How we use your information

  • To provide the service — serving the methodology, letting you resume sessions, and carrying your prior work into later sessions. (Legal basis: our contract with you.)
  • To improve the service — our team reviews session records, including the notes you provided, to see where a run went off track and make the methodology serve better. This review is internal to our team. Our people sometimes use AI tools to help, only under business terms that bar the provider from training on our data, so your content never becomes AI training data. (Legal basis: legitimate interest.)
  • To keep the service safe — automated detection of scraping and abuse. (Legal basis: legitimate interest.)
  • To respond to you when you contact us. (Legal basis: legitimate interest.)

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for advertising, and we show no ads.

The website

wayaframes.com is a static website hosted by Cloudflare. It collects three things:

  • What you send us. If you fill out a form or book a call, what you submit (typically your name and email) goes into our GoHighLevel account. We use it to respond to you and to send you email about Waya. Every email includes an unsubscribe link that works, and you can unsubscribe any time.
  • Analytics, only if you say yes. The site asks before it sets analytics cookies. If you click Accept, PostHog records how you use the site — pages viewed, clicks, and how far you scroll — so we can make it more useful. If you click Decline, no analytics run. The site remembers your choice in your browser.
  • Request logs. Cloudflare, which hosts the site, keeps standard request logs.

Some pages embed YouTube videos (in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode) or load fonts from Google Fonts. Those services receive the standard information your browser sends when it loads them, such as your IP address. The forms and booking pages are provided by GoHighLevel and may set their own cookies.

Browsers can send a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no common standard for honoring it, so the site does not change what it does when it receives one; analytics run only if you click Accept.

Signing into the services and using the website are independent — browsing the site creates no service account, and using the services adds nothing to the marketing systems.

Business outreach

We may contact you about Waya using business contact details from public professional sources or from business data providers. If you would rather not hear from us, reply or unsubscribe and we stop.

Where your information is processed

We are a US company and your information is processed in the United States. If you use Waya from the EU, UK, or elsewhere, your information is transferred to the US. Our providers maintain recognized transfer safeguards (Data Privacy Framework certification and/or Standard Contractual Clauses); details on request.

How long we keep it

Session content is your work product, so we keep it for the life of your account — that is what lets you return to past sessions. When you ask us to delete your account, we erase everything keyed to you within 30 days of verifying your request; residual copies in encrypted backups expire on a rolling schedule within a further 30 days. Website form submissions are kept until you ask us to remove them or unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us, at any time, to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you;
  • Export it (a machine-readable copy of everything keyed to your account);
  • Correct it;
  • Delete it — your account and all session content;
  • Object to a use of it, including the service-improvement review described above.

Email info@wayaframes.com. We will verify the request comes from the account holder, acknowledge it, and fulfill it within 30 days of verifying it.

If you are in the EU or UK, these are your GDPR rights and you also have the right to complain to your supervisory authority. If you are a California resident: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA, and the rights above match those the CCPA provides.

Security

The short version: encryption in transit and at rest, no public path into our databases (only our server, and our team through the database console, can reach them), separated staging and production environments, and every change reviewed before it ships. The full, plain-language account — including exactly what is stored and who can read it — is at How Waya Handles Your Data.

If a breach affecting your personal information ever occurs, we will notify affected users without undue delay, and within whatever deadline the law sets.

Children

Waya is for business use by adults. The services are not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the date at the top and, for material changes, notify account holders by email before the change takes effect.
