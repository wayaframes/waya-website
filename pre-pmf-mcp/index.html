The Challenge

Before product-market fit, everything is a question. Is this the right idea? Am I the right person to build it? What should I test first, and how would I even know if it worked? Most early founders answer these alone, in a blank chat window, with no method and no one who has walked the path.

The Pre-PMF MCP puts Waya's frameworks — our Wayaframes — inside the AI assistant you already use. Each one facilitates a proven method with you, step by step, and hands you a real deliverable at the end. It's the fastest honest route from "I have an idea" to "I have evidence."

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Pre-PMF MCP

What is the Pre-PMF MCP?

It's a Waya MCP — a small server you connect to your AI assistant — that adds a set of Wayaframes: guided, step-by-step frameworks the assistant runs with you. The free tier helps you surface your purpose, write a pre-PMF executive summary, and set your mission and values. Paid tiers add experiment design and a four-week coaching program.

What is a Wayaframe?

A Wayaframe is a facilitated framework that runs inside your AI assistant. Instead of a blank chat, the assistant walks you through a proven method — asking the right questions in the right order and producing a real deliverable at the end, like an Ikigai profile, an executive summary, or a set of experiments.

What do I get for free?

The free MCP includes three Wayaframes: personal Ikigai, the pre-PMF executive summary, and the mission and values guide. Together they help you clarify who you are as a founder, articulate the business you want to build, and set the foundation your company will scale on.

What does the $20 per month Pro tier add?

Pro adds the Waya experiment design Wayaframe, which turns your riskiest assumptions into small, testable experiments with clear success criteria — the engine that moves you toward product-market fit. It's billed monthly and includes everything in the free tier.

What is the $2,500 program?

It's a four-week program with four one-hour coaching sessions that set up your PMF infrastructure. A Waya specialist onboards you, aligns on your idea, helps you refine or eliminate hypotheses, configures your experiments, and gets your automations and analytics running — so you leave with a working system for finding product-market fit, not just a plan.

Who is this for?

Early-stage founders who are still searching for product-market fit — whether you're pre-idea, validating a hypothesis, or running your first experiments. If you know the destination is PMF but not yet the path, this is built for you.

What do I need to run it?

An AI assistant that supports MCP, such as Claude. You connect the Waya MCP once, and the Wayaframes become available as commands you can invoke any time.
