The Challenge

Before product-market fit, everything is a question. Is this the right idea? Am I the right person to build it? What should I test first, and how would I even know if it worked? Most early founders answer these alone, in a blank chat window, with no method and no one who has walked the path.

The Pre-PMF MCP puts Waya's frameworks — our Wayaframes — inside the AI assistant you already use. Over twelve weeks, Na'ama and Vince run them with you, one at a time, and each one ends in a real deliverable. It's the fastest honest route from "I have an idea" to "I have evidence."

Wayaframes

The four Wayaframes you work through

Each one runs inside your AI assistant. We take you through them in order during the workshop, and each ends in a document you keep.

Personal Ikigai

Surface what you love, what you're good at, what you can be paid for, and what the world needs — then synthesize the four intersections into a personal Ikigai statement. Clarity on why you're the founder to build this.

Business Case

Turn a rough idea into a sharp, one-page articulation of the business — the problem, the customer, the wedge, and the assumptions it rests on. The document you'll return to every time the idea shifts.

Mission & Values Guide

Set the foundation your company will scale on. Define the mission you're actually here for and the values that will guide the hard calls — before the pressure of growth makes them harder to change.

Experiment Design

Turn your riskiest assumptions into small, testable experiments with clear success criteria — the engine that actually moves you toward product-market fit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Pre-PMF MCP

What is the Pre-PMF MCP?

It's a Waya MCP — a small server you connect to your AI assistant — that adds a set of Wayaframes: guided, step-by-step frameworks the assistant runs with you. In the 12-week Product Market Fit Workshop you use it to surface your purpose, write a business case, set your mission and values, and design the experiments that get you to product-market fit.

What is a Wayaframe?

A Wayaframe is a facilitated framework that runs inside your AI assistant. Instead of a blank chat, the assistant walks you through a proven method — asking the right questions in the right order and producing a real deliverable at the end, like an Ikigai profile, a business case, or a set of experiments.

How is this different from just asking Claude or ChatGPT?

Waya doesn't replace your AI assistant. It runs inside it. A general assistant will talk through your idea for as long as you like, agree with most of it, and never tell you what to test first. The Wayaframes give it a set order: why you're the founder for this, what the business is, what it stands for, and which assumptions to test. Each one ends in a document you keep. Na'ama and Vince built them from scaling Cheetah to $150M in revenue, and in the workshop they coach you every week, which a chatbot can't do.

What is the Product Market Fit Workshop?

It's a $3,000, 12-week workshop that stands up your PMF infrastructure. Each week pairs a group session on the Waya OS modules with a private hour with Na'ama or Vince — from your first hypothesis to a running system of experiments, automations, and analytics, so you leave with a working system for finding product-market fit, not just a plan.

Who is this for?

Early-stage founders who are still searching for product-market fit — whether you're pre-idea, validating a hypothesis, or running your first experiments. If you know the destination is PMF but not yet the path, this is built for you.

What do I need to run it?

An AI assistant that supports MCP, such as Claude. We connect the Waya MCP with you at the start of the workshop. You also connect a couple of everyday tools — a knowledge base like Notion or Google Drive, and a meeting-transcription tool like Wispr Flow — so your learnings and customer calls flow into one place.
