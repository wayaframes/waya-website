Margins and Cash

Revenue is up. Margin isn't, and cash is tied up in the work.

How we approach it

Find where margin and cash leak out of the operation, fix the cause, and keep the gain.

  1. 1

    Gap analysis

    Measure the gap

    Put a cost on every step and a number on the cash tied up, against where you need to be.

    Used in: From 20% to 70% gross margin, Cutting the cash float 90%

  2. 2

    A3, root cause analysis

    Find the cause on the floor

    Walk the real process: double entry, inspecting every cart, paying suppliers before customers pay you.

    Used in: From 20% to 70% gross margin, Better quality with fewer QC staff

  3. 3

    Kaizen, standard work

    Build the fix into the system

    Change the process and the software together, then keep the metric visible so the gain holds.

    Used in: Better quality with fewer QC staff, From 20% to 70% gross margin

Results

Where we've fixed this

  • Restaurant AP automation Gross margin ~20% → ~70% We built invoice extraction into the app and moved from batches to one invoice at a time. Invoice with line items, all-in: $3.34 → $0.97 Line-item turnaround: 24 hours → under 30 minutes Read the case study
  • Cheetah · Warehouse Carts with a picking error ~72% → ~10% We tracked each picker's accuracy and checked only 1 in 10 carts for the most reliable pickers. Gross margin: ~10% → 20–25% QC staff per 100 orders: 7 FTEs → 2 FTEs Read the case study
  • Cheetah · Finance Cash fronted for product ~100% → ~8% We linked each customer payment to its supplier invoice, so the supplier was paid straight from it. Working capital freed: ~$1M Delivery to cash: 3–4 days → 1–3 days Read the case study
