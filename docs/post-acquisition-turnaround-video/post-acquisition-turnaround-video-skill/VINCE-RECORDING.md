# Vince: recording script for the Cheetah turnaround case study video

Hi Vince. This clip plays in a small square window (the "expert" window) in a roughly 80-second video about the
Cheetah post-acquisition turnaround. A narrator tells the story, and you are on screen four times, about 35 seconds
in all.

For three of those four appearances your clip plays **muted** under the narrator. What matters there is that you look
natural, talk to the lens and keep going long enough.

**One line is yours, in your own voice:** line **B**, the takeaway near the end. Please say it **word for word**.

## What we need
- **One continuous take of at least 50 seconds of you talking**, plus 3–4 seconds of quiet at each end. You are on
  screen about 35 seconds; with 30% margin that is about 46 seconds, so 50 is safe. Each take is about **60 seconds**.
- **2–3 takes.** We'll pick the warmest, and the best line B.
- **Pace:** relaxed and conversational, as if you were explaining it to a founder across a table. Pauses are fine.

## Where you appear (for reference)

| When | What's happening | About | Your audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | the narrator introduces you and Cheetah | 6 s | muted |
| Root cause | "On October twenty-second, Vince found the cause…" | 9 s | muted |
| Two tracks | track one by hand (with Na'ama sleeping at the warehouse), track two the system, barcode checks, standard work | 15 s | muted |
| The Waya lens | **you say line B** | 4–5 s | **yours** |

## The lines, in order

Read straight through. Lines 1–7 can be in your own words, since they play muted, but stay on these beats so your
face matches the story. **Line B must be word for word.**

**Start:** 3–4 seconds of quiet. Look at the lens, relaxed, with a small nod as if listening to a question.

1. "I'm Vince, co-founder of Waya. In 2022 I was at Cheetah Technologies. We had just bought three regional
   distributors, and rebuilt our warehouse to hold them."
2. "When the rebuilt warehouse went live, it fell apart. Customers got three items in four. We had pallets of fresh
   food going bad out back, and frozen pizza dough sitting out until the yeast woke up."
3. "I took over as COO. On October twenty-second I sat down to find the cause. It took a few hours, and it wasn't
   the crews. The building had changed. The warehouse system's logic never did."
4. "The crews fixed the building by day, and the system undid it by night."
5. "So we ran two tracks at once. Track one was by hand: the whole company on the floor, execs and engineers too,
   counting stock in the freezer. Na'ama was sleeping at the warehouse. Track two rebuilt the system's logic for the
   new building."
6. "Then we added barcode checks that stop mistakes, and wrote it into daily standard work, so it would survive the
   next reorg."
7. "By March the burn was down to about two hundred sixty thousand a month, and by the next October Cheetah was
   profitable."

Then pause a beat, look straight into the lens, and say:

8. **B (word for word, your own voice, about 4 seconds):**
   > "Separate the symptom from the cause, then work both at once."

   Say it calmly, as the one thing you'd want a founder to remember. Pause a beat after it.

**End:** 3–4 seconds of quiet. Hold eye contact, with a small nod or smile, as if the other person is answering.

**After the take:** say line B two more times on its own, with a pause and a fresh look into the lens before each.
That gives us a choice of reads.

Pronunciation: **Waya = WEIGH-ya.**

## Recording specs
- **Frame:** landscape (16:9) or square, **1080p or higher** (4K welcome), 25 or 30 fps.
- **Face centred**, eyes about one third down from the top, with **headroom and room on both sides**. We crop to a
  square around your face, so keep your head and shoulders well inside the middle of the frame. Don't lean out of it.
- **Camera at eye level**, about an arm's length or a bit more away. Look **into the lens**, not at the screen.
- **Background:** plain, a wall or a softly out-of-focus room. No busy patterns.
- **Light:** soft and in front of you, from a window in front or slightly to one side, or a lamp bounced off a wall.
  No bright window behind you.
- **Sound** (this matters now, because line B uses your audio):
  - A quiet room with soft furnishings, no echo.
  - No music, no TV, no fan or air conditioning running, phone on silent.
  - A lapel mic if you have one, clipped about a hand's width below your chin. Otherwise use the camera or phone mic,
    placed close.
- **Movement:** natural hand movement is good, but stay seated and centred.
- **Takes:** 2–3, each with the quiet start and end.

## Sending it
Send the original files to Oren, with no editing, no filters and no captions. If you used a separate mic, send the
audio file too.

A phone recording is fine if it meets the specs above: back camera preferred, locked exposure, on a stand.
