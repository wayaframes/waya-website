## Source summary
After buying three regional distributors (~$24M revenue), the company rebuilt its warehouse (new racking, freezer, hundreds of new locations) but never updated the warehouse management system's logic. Go-live in October 2022 broke fulfillment: fill rate 75%, shrink 17–18% (28% one week), on-time as low as 24%, 22% of orders with an error, ~$1M/month contribution-margin burn; pallets of food thrown out. Vince found the root cause on Oct 22: the crews fixed the building by day and the system undid it by night. Two tracks at once — the whole company on the floor by hand, and the slotting logic rebuilt underneath — then barcode QA hard-stops and daily standard work. Results: fill rate 99%+, shrink ~2%, errors ~3%, on-time ~90%+, burn to $260K/mo by March 2023, profitable by Oct 2023.
