How a pod plays

1

Gather a pod

Three to five kids of a similar age, a kitchen table, and one parent willing to read aloud.

2

Open the campaign

Each week's quest arrives with the parent's script, the puzzles, and the answer key. Zero prep.

3

Level up for real

Every solved puzzle levels a character. Every skill maps to a standard the kids are learning in school.
