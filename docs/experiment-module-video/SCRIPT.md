building Critical Character, a role-playing game that teaches kids to think, sold to learning pods and homeschool co-ops.

Then 12 weeks to fundable product-market fit with Na'ama and Vince, and the experiment cycle, round after round, to twelve
paying pods.
**Frame (Oren's layout rules):** every demo moment is the same three columns. Na'ama's widget top-left, which plays her clip

cycle, peaks on the customer call, pads only for the close. Mixed 10 dB under the voice.

`video.html` is the animation; `artifacts/` holds the real-looking pages (landing, survey, ChatGPT) and `artifacts/shoot.mjs`
screenshots them; `cycle/` is the experiment-cycle module with its own preview and stills script; `assets/logos/` the tool
marks and their manifest. The portrait cut has not been rebuilt for this edit; the site plays the landscape cut on phones
until it is.
