**Story:** Sam, 32, a new dad who spends his Sundays on client reports and wants to be with his son. On the side he is
building Critical Character, a role-playing game that teaches kids to think, sold to learning pods and homeschool co-ops.
Sixteen AI tools, a landing page nobody visited, a survey three family members answered, a chatbot that loved every idea.
Then 12 weeks to fundable product-market fit with Na'ama and Vince, and the experiment cycle, round after round, to twelve
paying pods.
**Frame (Oren's layout rules):** every demo moment is the same three columns. Na'ama's widget top-left, which plays her clip
once and then holds her still. A Claude screenshot centre. The Waya Tracker screenshot right. Nothing else.
**Voice:** a synthesized neural voice (edge-tts, en-US-AndrewNeural at +5%) as the placeholder for the real voiceover.
**Soundtrack:** composed in code; tense drone for the dark opening, lifts to F major as the screen warms, builds through the
cycle, peaks on the customer call, pads only for the close. Mixed 10 dB under the voice.

## Na'ama's clips

Drop them in as `assets/naama-1.mp4` (plays at 0:40, in the program scene) and `assets/naama-2.mp4` (plays at 2:23, at the
round-two Define). Up to six seconds each. The page plays them in her widget and `render/vo.mjs` lays their sound on the
voice track at the same moments. When `naama-2.mp4` exists, drop the voiced line "Then the next Define brings the ledger
back…" so she says it instead: delete that cue from the list in `video.html` and re-run the voice.

```

python render/music.py --dur 170.5
```

```bash
node render/mix.mjs --music -10 --duck 4
```

`video.html` is the animation; `artifacts/` holds the real-looking pages (landing, survey, ChatGPT) and `artifacts/shoot.mjs`
screenshots them; `cycle/` is the experiment-cycle module with its own preview and stills script; `assets/logos/` the tool
marks and their manifest. The portrait cut has not been rebuilt for this edit; the site plays the landscape cut on phones
until it is.
