Where we've fixed this

Cheetah · Support

From a 22-hour to a 6-minute support response

First response: 22 hours → 6 minutes

The problem

First response to a support ticket averaged 22 hours. Requests came in over five disconnected channels, and about half were resolved outside any system.

The diagnosis

A3, SIPOC, 5 Whys

There was no structured front door. A person had to read, sort and route every message, and the most common questions had no self-service path.

The fix

  • In-app intake: the customer picks the order and the issue, which creates a tagged ticket
  • Automatic routing to the right agent, with every channel feeding one system
  • Self-service for invoices, credits, returns and truck location, and agents allowed to approve small credits
  • First response22 hours → 6 minutes
  • Tickets assigned correctly, automatically10% → 95%
  • Tickets submitted in the app10% → 70%

Hospital · GI suite

Cutting a 5-month patient wait to 2

Referral to procedure wait: 5 months → 2 months

The problem

A two-room GI suite took about 29 minutes to turn a room over, capping each room near 10 procedures a day while more than 560 patients waited up to five months.

The diagnosis

A3, Kaizen, 5 Whys

Changeover was only part of it. Often the next case couldn't start because the physician was elsewhere or the next patient wasn't ready.

The fix

  • Standard work for room turnover, with supplies staged where each step needs them
  • A standard GI order set and recovery protocol in the records system
  • Pre-op readiness: IV placement priority, prep and fasting screening, a cleaner handoff
  • Referral to procedure wait5 months → 2 months (target was 3)
  • Room turnover29 min → 23.7 min
  • Procedures per room per day10.4 → 11.4
