Waya Support

Email: info@wayaframes.com — we aim to respond within two business days.

Getting help

Write to us for anything about the services: connecting Waya OS or Waya Pre-PMF to your AI assistant, sign-in trouble, a session that behaved unexpectedly, or billing questions if and when paid plans exist. Include the email address on your account and, if it concerns a specific session, roughly when it happened — that is enough for us to find it.

Data requests

You can ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your account and everything in it. Email info@wayaframes.com from the address on your account (or tell us how to verify you own it). We acknowledge every request and fulfill it within 30 days of verifying it. What each request covers is described in How Waya Handles Your Data; your full rights are in the Privacy Policy.

Deletion is permanent: your sessions and notes cannot be recovered afterward. We will say so and confirm before we act.

The documents

  • Privacy Policy — what we collect and your rights.
  • Terms of Service — the agreement governing use.
  • How Waya Handles Your Data — plain-language detail on storage, access, and security.
